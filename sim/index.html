About this simulation. This is not Amazon Alexa. The conversation is voiced by an AI language model (the model named by the server, via OpenRouter) acting as a simulated Alexa+ host; the spoken audio is your browser's own speech synthesis. What you say or type is sent to that model provider to generate the reply, and voice input uses your browser's speech recognition, which may send audio to the browser vendor. Reflection prompts come from a rule-based question bank and, when you give a birth profile, from a rule-based Four-Pillars (saju) calculator. It is for reflection and entertainment — not a prediction of the future, and not medical, psychological, legal, or financial advice. Journal entries are stored on the server under this browser's profile id until you delete them with the button above. Intended for ages 13 and up.
